# Liyu POS Demo Video Playbook

## Campaign

**Campaign name:** One Clear Shift  
**Tagline:** From order to payment, see the work clearly.  
**Primary goal:** Help a cafe, restaurant, bar, or tej house owner understand Liyu POS and request a guided demonstration.

Use a real demo account and real screens. Show only workflows that have been checked in the environment you are recording. Do not present sample figures, payment-provider status, customer stories, or offline behavior as verified business results. For any offline segment, confirm the workflow in a production browser before publishing it.

## Simple production approach

### Before recording

1. Prepare a clean demo business with sample products and a demo user. Avoid real customer, staff, or payment data.
2. Write down the exact path you will show. Close unrelated tabs and hide personal notifications.
3. Record the product screen at its normal size and zoom. Use short, deliberate actions; pause briefly after important results so viewers can read them.
4. Record narration separately in a quiet room if the screen recording has distracting background noise.

### Edit and export

- Make a vertical master at **1080 × 1920 (9:16)** for Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Keep titles and important controls away from the top and bottom interface overlays.
- Use direct cuts, restrained zooms, readable subtitles, and a consistent Liyu color treatment. Avoid decorative transitions that obscure the workflow.
- Keep the voice clear above the music. Use only music with a license that permits commercial use on the platforms and in the regions where you plan to publish.
- Export a clean MP4 master, then make a smaller Telegram-friendly copy while checking that screen text remains readable.
- Review every frame for names, phone numbers, business data, browser notifications, and any temporary errors before publishing.

## Follow-up series: learn the product and build a useful content library

Use the same structure for each clip: **one clear opening line → one real task → what the screen shows → a simple invitation to see a demo.** Keep most clips between 25 and 60 seconds. Publish two useful clips per week if that pace is sustainable.

| Episode | Topic | Demonstration | Accuracy note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A sale from start to receipt | Find a product, add it, review the bill, record payment, show receipt. | Use a supported payment method and a demo record. |
| 2 | From table order to service | Open a table, add items, save the order. | Show station routing only when configured and verified. |
| 3 | What the Kitchen or Bar sees | Show a real station ticket and its available status actions. | Do not imply instant real-time delivery if the queue refreshes by polling. |
| 4 | Stock comes in | Show the existing stock receiving workflow. | Use a demo product and explain only values visible in the application. |
| 5 | A handover records responsibility | Show who issues, who accepts, and where custody history appears. | Do not describe a handover as a sale or as a branch-stock deduction. |
| 6 | What changes after a sale | Show the sale and the resulting stock view after the authoritative update. | Do not imply an offline projection is server-confirmed. |
| 7 | The cashier’s shift | Show the existing shift screen and supported close or reconciliation path. | Publish only after the flow and report are verified. |
| 8 | A day from the owner’s view | Show existing dashboard or reporting screens. | Avoid invented totals, profit claims, or customer outcomes. |
| 9 | Roles in a restaurant | Briefly show waiter, cashier, and station tasks using appropriate demo accounts. | Do not imply a role can perform actions it is not authorized to perform. |
| 10 | Working through a connection interruption | Show a supported operation saved locally, pending sync, then confirmed after reconnection. | Record only after a production-browser offline test; state the limits clearly. |

### Four-week practice plan

- **Week 1 — Capture:** Record the main demo and two short task clips. Practice steady screen capture and concise narration.
- **Week 2 — Edit:** Add subtitles, remove pauses, and make one vertical export. Ask one person unfamiliar with the product what they understood.
- **Week 3 — Publish:** Share two clips on the channels where prospective local businesses already communicate with you. Reuse the same footage with platform-appropriate captions.
- **Week 4 — Improve:** Review watch completion, saves, replies, and demo requests. Change one element at a time, such as the opening line or clip length.

Treat these as learning signals, not proof of market demand. Track real inquiries and whether the business is a fit.

## Publishing checklist

- [ ] Every screen shown is from the real product.
- [ ] The exact workflow was tested in the environment represented by the video.
- [ ] No customer data, secrets, or personal notifications appear.
- [ ] Offline states are labeled as pending until server confirmation.
- [ ] Manually recorded digital payments are not described as provider-verified.
- [ ] Captions are readable and reviewed by a fluent speaker where translated.
- [ ] Music and graphics are cleared for commercial use on the selected platforms.
- [ ] The final call to action points to a contact channel that the business actually monitors.
- [ ] A viewer can understand what to do next without relying on the caption alone.
